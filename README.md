# 🤖 Layer 2: The Agentic Execution Pipeline

Welcome to the **Agentic Execution Layer** of the Alpaca AI Trading Agents Hackathon! This layer is the fully autonomous, multi-agent AI "brain" and "executor" of the project. 

It takes in market data, processes sentiment and technicals, formulates complex options strategies, mathematically validates the risks, and autonomously submits the trades to the Alpaca exchange.

## 🏗️ Architecture Flow

This pipeline operates as a true **Autonomous Decision Loop**, strictly passing data downstream.

## 🧩 Component Breakdown (A to Z)

### 1. 🔎 Market Scanner (The Funnel)
- **Role:** Finds and ranks promising assets.
- **Function:** It scans the broad market to find the best trading candidates based on momentum, trend, and volatility. It outputs a filtered list of high-conviction symbols for the downstream agents to investigate.

### 2. 📰 News Agent (The Sentiment Analyst)
- **Role:** Analyzes news and sentiment.
- **Function:** Pulls recent news headlines and articles for the scanned assets, then feeds them to an LLM to gauge sentiment. It identifies catalysts (e.g., earnings, product launches) that could drive immediate price action.

### 3. 🤖 Market Agent (The Technical Analyst)
- **Role:** Analyzes technical market conditions.
- **Function:** Consumes technical indicators (RSI, ATR, Moving Averages) and uses the Groq LLM to provide a directional bias (Bullish/Bearish/Neutral) for the asset.

### 4. 📊 Options Agent (The Quantitative Strategist)
- **Role:** Formulates options strategies & extracts OCC symbols.
- **Function:** 
  - Connects to Alpaca's `OptionHistoricalDataClient` to pull live options chains.
  - Filters out illiquid contracts (wide bid/ask spreads, low volume).
  - Uses the LLM to select one of 6 approved directional spread strategies (Long Call/Put, Bull/Bear Call Spread, Bull/Bear Put Spread).
  - Employs strict Python math to validate the LLM's selection against the real chain, extracting the 21-character OCC Symbol (e.g., `AAPL260925C00170000`).

### 5. 🧠 Decision Agent (The Portfolio Manager)
- **Role:** Combines all agent outputs into a final trading plan.
- **Function:** Ingests the Market, News, and Options outputs. If the agents are aligned (e.g., News is positive, Market is bullish, Options Agent found a viable call spread), the Decision Agent will issue a **TRADE** signal. If there are contradictions or hallucinations, it issues a **PASS**.

### 6. 🛡️ Risk Assessment Engine (The Chief Risk Officer)
- **Role:** Strictly validates the trade against portfolio risk rules.
- **Function:** Bypasses LLMs entirely and uses raw Python math to enforce "hard" institutional limits:
  - **Max Risk Per Trade:** Caps individual trade loss to a % of total equity.
  - **Portfolio Exposure:** Caps the total capital deployed in the market.
  - **Risk/Reward Minimum:** Rejects trades that don't offer at least a 1.5x payout.
  - **Auto-sizing:** Downsizes contract quantities to fit the risk limits perfectly.

### 7. 🚀 Execution Agent (The Trader)
- **Role:** Executes the live trade on Alpaca.
- **Function:** Reads the final approved Risk JSON. It constructs the precise Alpaca SDK payload (`MarketOrderRequest` or `LimitOrderRequest` for Multi-Leg Options) using the OCC symbols generated by the Options Agent, and instantly submits the live order to the Alpaca Paper Trading exchange.

## 🛡️ Hackathon Superpowers & Judging Criteria
- **Hallucination Proof:** By injecting deterministic Python math into the `Options Agent` and `Risk Assessment Engine`, the system immediately catches and kills trades if the LLM hallucinates an inverted spread, bad math, or fake strikes.
- **True Autonomy:** The entire pipeline runs autonomously without human intervention, checking off the primary hackathon mandate.
- **Hard Risk Guardrails:** Fully satisfies the hackathon judging criteria by proving the AI cannot overleverage the account or take uncompensated risks, thanks to the deterministic Risk Engine.
- **Full Alpaca Integration:** Natively embeds Alpaca's OptionHistoricalDataClient and TradingClient SDKs to handle the entire lifecycle of an options trade.
